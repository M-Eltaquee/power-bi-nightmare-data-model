About This Project

A telecom provider needed a clear view of where customer churn was concentrated — which contract types, payment methods, and service subscriptions were driving losses — and how much monthly revenue was at stake. This project analyzes a 7,043-customer telecom dataset to answer that question and turn it into prioritized, actionable recommendations.

Built as a 5-page Power BI report (Demographic Analysis, Account Details, Customer Subscription, Findings, Recommendations), with an Intro/start page in front of it. Followed a two-session, DAX-focused build: session 1 covered explicit measures and reused-measure patterns, session 2 covered page navigation, bookmarking, and the Findings/Recommendations pages.

Data Model & DAX Approach

Explicit measures throughout — nothing built on implicit drag-and-drop aggregation.

Core measures

MeasureLogic
Total CustomersCOUNT(customerID)
Churn CustomersCALCULATE([Total Customers], Churn = "Yes")
Churn Customers %DIVIDE([Churn Customers], [Total Customers])
Total Monthly RevenueSUM(MonthlyCharges)
Monthly Revenue LossCALCULATE([Total Monthly Revenue], Churn = "Yes") — reuses the existing revenue measure instead of rewriting the SUM
Revenue Loss %DIVIDE([Monthly Revenue Loss], [Total Monthly Revenue])

Grouped filters (e.g. multi-value payment-method comparisons) use the IN operator inside CALCULATE rather than nested ORs. Every measure was validated in a plain table visual before it went into a chart.

Page 4 — Main Findings

26.54% overall churn

1,869 of 7,043 customers — costing 139K in monthly revenue (30.5% of revenue).

Contract type is the strongest driver

42.71% churn for month-to-month vs 11.27% one-year, 2.83% two-year.

Tenure gap

Churned customers average 17.98 months vs 37.57 for retained.

Support tickets track churn

Large gap between churned and retained ticket volume (see Known Issues for which ticket type).

Electronic check payers churn most

45.29% vs 15–19% for automatic payment methods.

Fiber optic churns highest

41.89% vs 18.96% DSL, 7.40% no internet.

Add-ons roughly halve churn

No security/backup/device-protection customers churn ~2x subscribers.

Charges reflect tenure, not price

Churned pay more monthly ($74 vs $61) but less total ($1,532 vs $2,555).

Page 5 — Recommendations

Move month-to-month customers to longer contracts — targeted 1–2 year offersHigh

Known Issues

Tickets page — chart titles likely swapped. "Tech Tickets" chart shows a flat 0.53/0.47 split; "Admin Tickets" chart shows a wide 1.16/0.15 split. Against the raw totals (Tech: 2,955 · Admin: 3,632) and the original build, the wide gap belongs to Tech tickets. Fix: swap the two chart titles.

Payments page — titles not updated. Both charts were showing "Average Tenure (in Months)" instead of "Paperless Billing" and "Payment Method" — leftover text from copying the Tenure page layout.

Main Services page — Multiple Lines mislabeled as Phone Service. The second "Phone Service" chart's categories (No / Yes / No phone service — 3,390 / 2,971 / 682) match the Multiple Lines field, not Phone Service, which only has Yes/No. Title needs correcting.

Additional Services page — Tech Support missing. The page shows Online Backup, Online Security, and Device Protection, but the fourth panel duplicates the Internet Service chart from the Main Services page instead of showing Tech Support — one of the strongest churn signals in the dataset (41.64% No vs 15.17% Yes in the original build).
